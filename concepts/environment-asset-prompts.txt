SHORELINE

Palms have natural irregular crowns and fine individual fronds, broadleaf trees fill the spaces beneath, distant layered vegetation adds depth. A few tiny amber lamps visible deep among foliage. Sun just set behind the left side of the treeline, warm edge light, cool shadow detail. No mountains, no surreal island, no repetitively spaced palm trees, no fog wall. Natural photographic exposure, very detailed vegetation, realistic color, cinematic but subtle. Image is a clean panorama asset, not a website mockup. Match the atmosphere of the attached houseboat concept, but remove the entire boat, water foreground and all typography. Only sky and distant shoreline, composed as specified.
